---
title: Light & Dark
description: Both modes are built at once; you edit one at a time.
---

Tonex builds light and dark together from the same source. You edit one mode at a time, and the engine keeps the other coherent. Toggle the active mode with `D`.

## Shared across modes

These choices apply to both modes at once:

- The **seed**
- The **variant** and the CMF second source
- **Palette overrides** and **custom colors**
- **Chart** settings

Change any of these and both modes update together.

## Per mode

These are set independently for light and dark:

- **Contrast** level
- **Surface** adjustment, including the tint text accent
- **Role bindings** and **role overrides** for shadcn

This split lets you push contrast harder in dark mode, or tint dark surfaces more than light ones, without disturbing the other mode.

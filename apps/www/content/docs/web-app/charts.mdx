---
title: Charts
description: Generate an accessible color series for data visualization.
---

Tonex generates a color series for charts and data visualization alongside the main system, derived from the same seed. You choose how the series is built in **Settings**.

## Series styles

- **Shades of one hue** — a single hue stepped across several tones. Cohesive, and the natural choice for sequential data like a heatmap or a single trend.
- **Multi-hue rotation** — a set of related hues rotated around your seed. A middle ground when you want variety that still reads as one family.
- **Fully distinct** — several clearly separate hues. The choice for categorical data where each series must be told apart at a glance.

## Color vision

The chart colors are checked against common forms of color-vision deficiency, so the series stays distinguishable for more readers. For an achromatic seed with almost no color, Tonex still produces a colored series rather than a row of greys.

## Export

Chart colors are an opt-in toggle when you export. See [Export](/docs/web-app/export).

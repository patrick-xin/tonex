## A color I changed snapped back

A [binding](/docs/web-app/shadcn/role-bindings) tracks the system, so it moves when the seed, variant, or contrast changes. If you need a color to stay put, [pin it with an override](/docs/web-app/shadcn/role-overrides) instead.

## Why doesn't primary match the color I picked?

It's still your color. What makes a color recognisably *yours* — its hue and how vivid it is — carries through to every role, `primary` included. The one thing that shifts is its lightness: each role is re-toned lighter or darker so the text and surfaces paired with it stay readable. So `primary` isn't a different color from your seed; it's your color adapted for the job of being a primary, sitting at the lightness that keeps it legible. That re-toning is the contrast guarantee doing its work, not your color getting lost.

When you truly need the exact value you picked — pixel for pixel — that's what the brand override is for. It pins your color as-is and lets Tonex generate a foreground that stays readable on top. It's the rare exception for when an exact match matters, not the everyday way to reach for a role. See [Limitations](/docs/reference/limitations#your-seed-isnt-your-primary-color) for more on why the roles are tuned this way.

## An edit didn't carry to the other mode

Some controls are per mode by design: contrast, surfaces, bindings, and overrides. Switch modes with `D` and make the matching change there. See [Light & dark](/docs/web-app/light-dark).

## The hue control is off

Your seed is close to grey, so there's no hue to adjust. See [Limitations](/docs/reference/limitations).

## A contrast pair is flagged

Tonex reports contrast but doesn't force it — a flag is information, not a block. Raise the [contrast](/docs/key-concepts/contrast) level, or pin the affected role to a value that passes.

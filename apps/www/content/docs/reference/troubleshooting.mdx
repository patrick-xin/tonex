## A color I changed snapped back

A [binding](/docs/web-app/shadcn/role-bindings) tracks the system, so it moves when the seed, variant, or contrast changes. If you need a color to stay put, [pin it with an override](/docs/web-app/shadcn/role-overrides) instead.

## Why doesn't primary match the color I picked?

The palette is still authored from your color, but `primary` is a role, not a promise to echo the seed exactly.

What makes the color recognisably yours — hue and chroma — carries through to the palette. What can shift is tone: Tonex may make a role lighter or darker so the text and surfaces paired with it stay readable. That re-toning is the contrast guarantee doing its work, not your color getting lost.

When you truly need the exact value you picked — pixel for pixel — use the brand pair. It keeps your seed as-is and adds a generated foreground that stays readable on top. It's the exception for exact-color surfaces, not the everyday way to reach for a role.

See [Limitations](/docs/reference/limitations#your-seed-isnt-your-primary-color) for more on why roles are tuned this way.

## An edit didn't carry to the other mode

Some controls are per mode by design: contrast, surfaces, bindings, and overrides. Switch modes with <Kbd>D</Kbd> and make the matching change there. See [Light & dark](/docs/web-app/light-dark).

## The hue control is off

Your seed is close to grey, so there's no hue to adjust. See [Limitations](/docs/reference/limitations).

## A contrast pair is flagged

Tonex reports contrast but doesn't force it — a flag is information, not a block. Raise the [contrast](/docs/key-concepts/contrast) level, or pin the affected role to a value that passes.

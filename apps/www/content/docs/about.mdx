---
title: About
description: Why Tonex exists, and who makes it.
---

I built Tonex because doing color well by hand is stressful. One wrong token and the whole interface feels off, so most of us play it safe and reach for the same neutrals. I wanted a tool that made it safe to use your actual color — where the system holds together for you, so being distinct stops feeling like a risk.

The idea is simple: give it one color, get a complete, accessible system that's coherent by construction. Change a digit and you get a different one. Your identity is a point in a space, not a preset everyone shares.

Tonex's engine, `@tonex/core`, is developed in the open. The aim was never to beat anyone — it's to make confident color the default, for people who'd otherwise ship gray.
